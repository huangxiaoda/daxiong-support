隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

我们非常重视您的隐私。本隐私政策旨在向您说明我们如何收集、使用和保护您的信息。

We take your privacy very seriously. This Privacy Policy is designed to explain how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. 我们收集的信息 / Information We Collect

在您使用《大熊学武功》时，我们可能会收集以下非个人身份信息，以改善游戏体验：

When you use "Bear Learns Kung Fu", we may collect the following non-personally identifiable information to improve the gaming experience:

本地数据：您的游戏进度（如最高分、通关等级）、本地设置（如音量、语言偏好）。这些数据仅存储在您的设备本地。

Local Data: Your game progress (e.g., high scores, levels) and local settings (e.g., volume, language preferences). This data is stored only locally on your device.

支付信息：当您进行内购时，所有支付处理均由 Apple 官方 StoreKit 框架完成。我们不会收集、存储或访问您的任何信用卡或支付账号信息。

Payment Information: When you make in-app purchases, all payment processing is handled by Apple's official StoreKit framework. We do not collect, store, or access any of your credit card or payment account information.

2. 第三方服务 / Third-Party Services

本应用使用 Apple 的应用内购买服务（In-App Purchase）。Apple 可能会收集您与交易相关的数据，详情请参阅 Apple 的隐私政策。

The App uses Apple's In-App Purchase service. Apple may collect data related to your transactions. For details, please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. 数据安全 / Data Security

由于本应用为单机游戏，您的购买凭证和解锁状态均安全地存储在您的设备本地沙盒中。我们没有服务器会保存您的个人记录。

Because this App is a standalone offline game, your purchase receipts and unlock status are stored securely within your device's local sandbox. We do not have servers that save your personal records.

4. 儿童隐私 / Children's Privacy

本应用不会有意向 13 岁以下的儿童收集个人信息。

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
