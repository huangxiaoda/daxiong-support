隐私政策

我们非常重视您的隐私。本隐私政策旨在向您说明我们如何收集、使用和保护您的信息。

1. 我们收集的信息

在您使用《大熊学武功》时，我们可能会收集以下非个人身份信息，以改善游戏体验：

  • 本地数据：您的游戏进度（如最高分、通关等级）、本地设置（如音量、语言偏好）。这些数据仅存储在您的设备本地。
  • 支付信息：当您进行内购时，所有支付处理均由 Apple 官方 StoreKit 框架完成。我们不会收集、存储或访问您的任何信用卡或支付账号信息。

2. 第三方服务

本应用使用 Apple 的应用内购买服务（In-App Purchase）。Apple 可能会收集您与交易相关的数据，详情请参阅 Apple 的隐私政策。

3. 数据安全

由于本应用为单机游戏，您的购买凭证和解锁状态均安全地存储在您的设备本地沙盒中。我们没有服务器会保存您的个人记录。

4. 儿童隐私

本应用不会有意向 13 岁以下的儿童收集个人信息。

Privacy Policy

We take your privacy very seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII). The App only stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Game Data: Your game progress (e.g., high scores, levels) and local settings (e.g., volume, language preferences).
  • Payment Information: All in-app purchases are processed securely by Apple's StoreKit framework. We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card or Apple ID payment information.

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses Apple's In-App Purchase service. Apple may collect data related to your transactions. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for more details.

3. Data Security

Because this is an offline single-player game, your purchase receipts and unlock status are stored securely within your device's local sandbox. We do not maintain servers that store your personal records.

4. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
